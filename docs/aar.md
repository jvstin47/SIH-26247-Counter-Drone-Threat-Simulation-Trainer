# V5 After-Action Review

AAR is generated from the persisted completed session record: score breakdown, assessment rows, event timeline, strengths/mistakes, confidence calibration and replay/keyframe data. AAR can be opened from the last session, session history and analytics, and exported as JSON.

Hidden ground truth is exposed only in the review/replay context. Normal training presents observations and uncertainty. AAR narrative/coaching is deterministic and cannot alter the saved score or ground truth.
